Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a replacement for my doctor?

No. NutriMama AI provides educational information only. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before making medical decisions — especially around symptoms, supplements, or existing conditions.

How does the AI decide what to tell me?

A Supervisor Agent routes your question to specialized agents (nutrition, medical guidelines, food safety, etc.), which pull from your profile and, where relevant, a retrieval-based knowledge base — rather than one model guessing at everything.

What happens if I mention a concerning symptom?

Emergency detection runs on every message before anything else. If it looks like you might be describing a medical emergency, you'll be directed to contact your provider or emergency services immediately — no meal plan or nutrition advice will be generated instead.

Is my data private?

Your profile and chat history are tied to your account and protected behind authentication. See our Privacy Policy for details.
